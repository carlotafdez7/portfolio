I’m a graphic designer and illustrator with a strong passion for photography, especially analog processes. My work combines visual identity, illustration and image-making, with a focus on thoughtful composition and clear visual language. Alongside my design practice, I explore both analog and digital photography as a way to enrich my creative perspective.

My skill set includes advanced use of Adobe Creative Suite across graphic design, illustration, photography and motion graphics. I regularly work with tools such as Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and After Effects, applying them to create coherent visual systems, animated pieces and photographic projects.

Book collection cover design

This project combines illustration and editorial design to create three covers for a self-help book collection. Each cover visually represents the main idea of its narrative through clear composition, strong typography and a cohesive illustrated style.

Muid magazines

Editorial design of a magazine collection for a university. The set includes three issues, one for each degree: graphic design, industrial design and fashion design. Illustration

Character design · Editorial illustration · Visual storytelling

Tadeo the juggler

“Tadeo the Juggler” is an illustrated children’s book that combines editorial design and illustration to tell a story about patience, creativity and the learning process. The project explores how visual storytelling can communicate emotions and ideas beyond words, using illustration as a key narrative element rather than a purely decorative one.

The relationship between image, text and layout was carefully developed to create a clear and engaging reading experience. Through thoughtful composition, color choices and pacing, the book builds a coherent visual language that supports the story and guides the reader naturally through each page.

Stanley Kubrick book cover series

This project explores illustration as a narrative and conceptual tool applied to book cover design. The series reinterprets iconic films by Stanley Kubrick through a strong graphic language, using symbolism, limited color palettes and textured finishes inspired by print and analog processes. Each cover is designed to visually translate the core themes of the story, creating a cohesive yet distinctive collection that balances illustration, typography and editorial composition.

Branding

Identity systems · Logo design · Visual direction

Lattíssimo

Lattíssimo is a coffee brand that focuses on elegance, warmth and sensory experience. The visual identity combines organic patterns, earthy tones and refined typography to convey a premium yet approachable character.

The branding system is applied across different formats such as packaging, capsules and takeaway cups, ensuring visual coherence and reinforcing the brand’s values of quality, craftsmanship and everyday enjoyment.

Brand Identity Project Two

Development of a complete corporate identity for MUID, a design university focused on creativity, innovation and contemporary visual culture. The project includes the design of posters, signage and institutional materials, creating a flexible visual system that adapts to different degrees while maintaining a strong, recognizable identity.
